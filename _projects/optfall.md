Flesh and Blood has no rulings lookup of any kind. Settling a question means
reading the Comprehensive Rules on your phone, or asking a Discord of several
thousand people. Optfall optimises for the one thing nobody else does: being
right.

Every card page joins the card to the rules that govern it, and every rule
back to the cards that use it. Legality is versioned and citable. Search
speaks the syntax players already know from the official Card Vault, and
extends it to the rules.

Nothing it serves is generated prose. Every answer is a parsed official
document or a named human author, so a confidently wrong ruling isn't a risk
to manage. It's a thing the tool can't do.
