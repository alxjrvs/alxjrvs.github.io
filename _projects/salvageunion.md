Salvage Union is a game about scavenging mechs in a ruined world, and its rules
deserve better than a PDF you scroll through mid-session. SalvageUnion.io is
the full SRD as a fast, searchable reference, with a builder for pilots and
mechs.

The data underneath is published as structured JSON, so it's become the layer
other tools read from: InTheUnionNow keeps your pilots and mechs in your
browser, and a Discord bot rolls and looks things up without leaving your
table's server.
